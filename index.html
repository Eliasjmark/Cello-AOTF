    The left side of the screen moves Cello, the right side moves the camera. The buttons in the lower right are sword, jump, roll, lock on, shield, items and map. Items you put on a button get their own button at the top.
    
      
    The first click captures the mouse, Esc releases it. C walk, M sound, N music, P Retro mode. Gamepad: A jump, X sword, B roll, RT shield, LT lock on, Y talk, RB, LB and D-pad right items, Start item menu, Back map.
